## Risks

- Severe legal and patent litigation from major pharmaceutical companies (such as Eli Lilly) who own the patents for GLP-1 drugs.
  - "surely you'll get sued by the GLP-1 patent owners like HIMs did if you sell your own product?" ([HN comment by make3](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49543530))
- Regulatory shutdown if the FDA determines that brand-name drug manufacturers can meet the market demand, ending the legal window for compounding.
  - "Are you at risk of being shut down on a dime if the FDA decides Lilly can meet demand?" ([HN comment by colingauvin](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49543530))
- Low barriers to entry for competitors who can easily set up drop-shipped compounding pharmacies with minimal capital.
  - "I ran the numbers and 50k is is all you need to get a service agreement with a compounding pharmacy" ([HN comment by rootsudo](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49543530))
