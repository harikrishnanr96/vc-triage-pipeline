## Team

Pietro and Luigi are the co-founders of Manufact, previously known as mcp-use, who have been building open-source SDKs and tools for the Model Context Protocol (MCP) since April 2025.

- "Hi HN, we are Pietro and Luigi, cofounders of Manufact ( https://manufact.com ), a cloud for MCP apps and servers." ([HN post](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48762862))
- "We have been working on MCP since April 2025." ([HN post](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48762862))

## Product

Manufact is an MCP vertical cloud platform that provides hosting, cross-client testing, analytics, and publishing submission packs for Model Context Protocol (MCP) apps and servers, built by the creators of the open-source mcp-use SDKs.

- "Manufact is an MCP vertical cloud designed for dev teams putting MCP Apps and servers in production." ([HN post](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48762862))
- "Manufact (formerly mcp-use) is a platform for deploying, testing, observing, and publishing MCP servers and MCP apps." ([website](https://manufact.com))

## Risks

- S skepticism over whether MCP is a durable protocol or a passing architectural fad compared to standard REST APIs or direct tool use.
  - "MCP is a deadend. CLI use is the future." ([HN comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48762862))
  - "Saying MCPs are the new websites is like saying "SOAP" is the new websites, or "REST" is the new websites." ([HN comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48762862))
- Platform risk where major AI host providers might build native publishing and testing utilities directly into their ecosystems.
  - "OpenAI and Anthropic review and approve their own listings." ([website](https://manufact.com))
